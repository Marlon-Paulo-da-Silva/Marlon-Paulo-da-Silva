### About me

Back-end engineer at [ImobiBrasil](https://www.linkedin.com/company/imobibrasil/), building the platform real estate agencies use to run their websites, leads and sales.

- **Day to day:** PHP, Laravel, MySQL, REST APIs, Docker, performance and tracking (Meta, TikTok, Clarity pixels).
- **Now:** applying AI where it moves a number: WhatsApp automations, lead routing, n8n flows and LLM agents for small businesses.
- **Also:** I document what works (and what doesn't) with AI in business on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/marlonpaulo.ia).
- **Education:** Systems Analysis and Development, Fatec Presidente Prudente.
